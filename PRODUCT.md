# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary visitors are drag-racing fans and curious Scandinavians who want the story of Anders Edh and The Beast. Secondary visitors are potential sponsors and partners evaluating brand fit. Both arrive story-first: emotion and credibility come before pitch decks or contact forms. Context is often evening browsing on phone or laptop, looking for atmosphere, proof of performance, and a sense of who this team is.

## Product Purpose

EDH Racing’s public site exists to make the garage-to-Santa-Pod arc feel like one continuous drag pass: staging, launch, pass, chutes, then a calm partner invite. Success looks like: visitors feel tyngd and respect, understand the handmade Hudiksvall path to 5.74 s @ 415 km/h, and sponsors arrive already emotionally aligned. The site is bilingual (EN + SV) and ships as a Vite + React experience, not a CMS brochure.

## Brand Personality

tyngd · precision · återhållsamhet · underdog

Voice is Scandinavian, spare, and humble. Anders is a garage underdog who happens to run at the sharp end: never boast, never pose as a polished pro brand. Let times, photos and craft speak. High-end cinematic polish over spectacle. Emotional arc: still staging → violent pass → quiet chutes → partner invite. No shouty racing neon; power is implied through rhythm, imagery, and restraint. Avoid self-congratulating words (elite, excellence, extreme, spectacular) when describing the person or the team.

## Anti-references

- Generic racing neon, cyan/magenta glow, and “need for speed” game UI
- Template AI landing pages (hero metrics, identical icon cards, gradient text)
- Messy display fonts (Speed Rusher / Aristotele era) and italic display headings
- Soft SaaS cards, pill clusters, and brochure section stacks
- Blur-heavy reveals and motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Design Principles

1. **One drag pass, not a brochure** — every page beat should map to staging → launch → pass → chutes → invite.
2. **Show feeling through rhythm and image, not more copy** — atmosphere before explanation.
3. **Story first, sponsors through emotion** — partner CTAs earn their place after the pass lands.
4. **Precision over effect** — machined details, locked fonts, intentional motion (transform/opacity only).
5. **Scandinavian stillness around extreme speed** — quiet frames make the violence hit harder.
6. **Underdog, not peacock** — Anders does not brag. Copy states facts and garage roots; the visitor draws the conclusion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast where feasible on the dark brand base. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Gate hover affordances to fine pointers. Keep focus states visible. Avoid motion that relies on blur or vestibular-heavy effects. Bilingual content must remain equivalent in EN and SV.
