## Learned User Preferences

- Prefers Swedish in conversation and product copy; the site must ship bilingual EN + SV.
- Keep the existing Vite + React stack; do not migrate to Next.js from the blueprint.
- MVP first (seven pages, design system, copy, static data); phase 2 includes Pass Arena (PlayCanvas) and interactive chassis.
- MVP contact should use mailto (zero setup); Formspree/Resend or similar can come later.
- Rejected Speed Rusher and Aristotele as too messy; prefer clean condensed webfonts (Oswald display, Barlow Condensed UI, DM Sans body) without italic display headings.
- Visual makeovers should stay on-brand (dark base, primary blue, locked fonts); prefer high-end cinematic polish over generic racing neon.
- Motion should stay intentional and performant: GPU `transform`/`opacity` only, hover gated to fine pointers, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid blur-heavy reveals.
- Design north star: the whole site should feel like one drag pass (staging → launch → pass → chutes → partner invite); weigh choices against tyngd, precision, and återhållsamhet; reject scroll-dots, custom cursors, and glassmorphism cards; Journey/Resan is one interactive system — a running timeslip career story (cause and effect, Anders’ voice, successes and setbacks), not a chapter list, chart, or filterable database; reject Journey filter chips, hero ET countdown, everyday-anchor comparison cards, duplicate blank timeslips, and closing chart replay.
- Voice: Anders as underdog — humble garage builder, never boastful or “pro brand”; let times and craft speak.
- For the car 360, prefer a lightweight frame-based turntable over Three.js / WebGL; Pass Arena is the separate PlayCanvas 3D feature.
- Home hero should be logo-led and sparse (EDH logo, short CTAs to Journey/Machine, full-bleed video + local scrim, GSAP entrance); soft fade from hero into the next section (not a hard edge); keep Home cinematic end-to-end rather than a dashboard of identical cards — stats should read as the pass, never flash `0,00` while counting up; HomeTeam features John (not Marie) with the active crew; Home Anders image-led with sparse copy and Anders in front of overlapping layers using `standinganders` (do not swap Home Anders/team plates unless asked); Results is a season story (calendar/next start first, then 201 m times, Santa Pod 5.74 and EDRS places quiet — no opening 5.74 hero, no podium theatre); Home keeps the cinematic 5.74 teaser; avoid empty dark voids and card-heavy SaaS grids on Home.
- Pass Arena: stage zooms into start, then follow-car or wide zoom-out; drive motion from authentic timeslip splits (not fake mid-strip stalls or jerky gear-like motion); keep the HUD small and never covering the car, with an expandable time card that opens to explain Reaction / 60' / 330' / 1/8 / 1000' / 1320' ET and trap speed via everyday anchors (201 m vs 402 m); frame it as a dual-lane drag race by letting users pick an opponent (passenger plane, F1, etc.) and running the pass until the last car finishes, with dual-lane scoreboard times like a real strip; finish continues into the chutes so the car stays visible (parachutes, scoreboard, result after ~1 s); let users pre-race inspect and rotate the car; prefer clear camera choreography, light daytime strip atmosphere (not a dark-grey or all-blue sky), and populated grandstands with a real crowd over overdone speed VFX or empty/lego-like crowds; no sponsor logos in the 3D scene until they map to named body meshes.
