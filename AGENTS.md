## Learned User Preferences

- Prefers Swedish in conversation and product copy; the site must ship bilingual EN + SV; when the user pastes SV copy, treat it as canonical and mirror it in EN.
- Keep the existing Vite + React stack; do not migrate to Next.js from the blueprint.
- MVP first (seven pages, design system, copy, static data); phase 2 includes Pass Arena (PlayCanvas) and interactive chassis.
- MVP contact should use mailto (zero setup); Formspree/Resend or similar can come later.
- Rejected Speed Rusher and Aristotele as too messy; prefer clean condensed webfonts (Oswald display, Barlow Condensed UI, DM Sans body) without italic display headings.
- Visual makeovers should stay on-brand (dark base, primary blue, locked fonts); prefer high-end cinematic polish over generic racing neon.
- Motion should stay intentional and performant: GPU `transform`/`opacity` only, hover gated to fine pointers, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid blur-heavy reveals.
- Design north star: the whole site should feel like one drag pass (staging → launch → pass → chutes → partner invite); weigh choices against tyngd, precision, and återhållsamhet; reject scroll-dots, custom cursors, and glassmorphism cards; Journey/Resan is one interactive system — a running timeslip career story (cause and effect, Anders’ voice, successes and setbacks), not a chapter list, chart, or filterable database; reject Journey filter chips, hero ET countdown, everyday-anchor comparison cards, duplicate blank timeslips, and closing chart replay.
- Voice: Anders as underdog — humble garage builder, never boastful or “pro brand”; let times and craft speak.
- Machine (`Maskinen`) shows the Camaro in a dedicated PlayCanvas studio viewer (`CamaroStudio`: same GLB/paint as Pass, dark plate, idle orbit, drag yaw, `prefers-reduced-motion` locks a 3/4 view) — not the photo turntable, not Pass inspect/strip, and not a second engine (Three.js / `<model-viewer>`); WebGL fallback is a still 3/4 photo, not the old slideshow.
- Home hero should be logo-led and sparse (EDH logo, short CTAs to Journey/Machine, full-bleed video + local scrim, GSAP entrance); soft fade from hero into the next section (not a hard edge); keep Home cinematic end-to-end rather than a dashboard of identical cards — stats should read as the pass, never flash `0,00` while counting up; HomeTeam features John (not Marie) with the active crew; Home Anders image-led with sparse copy and Anders in front of overlapping layers using `standinganders` (do not swap Home Anders/team plates unless asked); Results is a season story (calendar/next start first, then 201 m times, Santa Pod 5.74 and EDRS places quiet — no opening 5.74 hero, no podium theatre); Home keeps the cinematic 5.74 teaser labeled Rekordpasset (not Simulatorn); SEO/metadata should lead with Top Doorslammer / Camaro, not “Hudiksvall till Europatoppen”; avoid empty dark voids and card-heavy SaaS grids on Home.
- Pass Arena is an interactive reconstruction of the 5.7451 Santa Pod record pass, not a driving game: Christmas-tree staging then launch; choreographed cameras with the Camaro as hero and planted on the strip (no free-cam mid-pass, no flying car, horizon above the asphalt); drive motion from authentic timeslip splits (not fake mid-strip stalls); keep the HUD small and never covering the car; opponents (F1, airliner) as discreet ghosts rather than full cars on the start line; finish holds on 5.7451 / 415 km/h with the stripe and time visible (no blocking wall); then an authentic timeslip (racing terms R/T · 60 FT · 1/8 ET, reaction excluded from ET, 5.7451 and 415 as the hero — not an educational modal); pre-race inspect/rotate; light daytime strip with rubber groove and a real crowd over overdone speed VFX; no sponsor logos in the 3D scene until they map to named body meshes.
